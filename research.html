Research Interests

My research focuses on how large-scale sociotechnical systems shape learning, behavior, and decision-making. I am particularly interested in how data, algorithms, and institutional structures interact in educational and applied settings. This includes studying online learning environments, AI-mediated tutoring systems, and information-rich platforms where human and machine systems jointly influence outcomes.

I am especially interested in how these systems can be analyzed through data-driven and network-based methods to understand learning processes at scale, with applications ranging from formal education to informal digital environments.

PLUS Research Assistant (PLUS Lab, Carnegie Mellon University)

As a Graduate Research Assistant with PLUS at Carnegie Mellon University, I work on research led by Conrad Borchers (PhD student in Human-Computer Interaction Institute) within the broader PLUS research initiative directed by Ken Koedinger. PLUS focuses on developing and evaluating AI-supported learning systems that help students improve mathematics performance through goal setting and personalized tutoring.

My work focuses on understanding how these systems affect student learning at scale in real classroom environments. I study how behavioral data from students interacting with hybrid human-AI tutoring tools can be used to model learning outcomes and improve instructional design.

My responsibilities include building and maintaining data pipelines for large educational datasets, cleaning and structuring survey and assessment data, and developing teacher-facing dashboards that translate student data into actionable insights. I also support classroom-based data collection in Pittsburgh-area schools, including administering diagnostic assessments.

In addition, I contribute to machine learning analyses evaluating the impact of PLUS's hybrid tutoring system on student performance and engagement. This work sits at the intersection of learning sciences, AI systems, and educational data science.

Seeds Development Trialing Intern (Syngenta)

As a Seeds Development Trialing Intern with Syngenta, I worked on large-scale soybean field trials supporting data-driven crop development. I integrated phenotypic measurements, UAV imagery, and GPS data across multiple trial sites and collaborated with data scientists on predictive models for plant performance.

My responsibilities included building data cleaning workflows, validating drone-based measurements against field observations, and supporting trial reporting used in product advancement decisions. I also conducted UAV-based field imaging after obtaining an FAA Part 107 license.

This role provided experience working with complex agricultural data systems that connect field environments, remote sensing, and predictive modeling in R&D settings.

PRO-DAIRY Research Assistant (Cornell University)

As a summer Research Assistant with Cornell PRO-DAIRY under Jason Oliver, I collected and analyzed field data on how dairy manure management systems affect methane emissions. The work involved integrating environmental measurements and field observations to support research on agricultural sustainability.

I contributed to data cleaning workflows, structured multi-source environmental datasets, and supported field data collection across dairy operations. This experience strengthened my interest in applying data systems approaches to environmental and agricultural problems.
